02
Context // 02

The Boxy
Prison.

Traditional habit trackers feel like spreadsheets—rigid, punishing, and clinical. The emotional friction of "missing a day" often leads to total abandonment.

"I feel guilty looking at my old tracker. Those red boxes feel like a report card I'm failing."
— Sarah, Freelancer
"Most apps feel like work. I want tracking my habits to feel like a break."
— James, Creative Lead
03
Solution // 03

A Botanical
Atmosphere.

Routiq replaces clinical metrics with organic growth. Habits are visualized as plants; sessions as water. Failure is not a 'Red Box', but a season of stillness.

Punishing "X" marksGleaming morning dew
