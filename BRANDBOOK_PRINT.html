Project snapshot

Routiq is a habit-tracking product built around reflection, milestones, rewards, plant growth, and atmospheric progress visuals.

Core shift

Move away from white-box productivity tooling toward a richer interface where routine feels expressive, calm, and lived-in.

Primary outcome

A unified system spanning landing, dashboard, registry, garden, reports, dark mode, and documentation.

Overview02
Context

The challenge and the opportunity.

ProblemMost habit tools feel sterile, generic, and emotionally empty.
TensionUsers want structure, but not at the cost of warmth or motivation.
OpportunityTurn routine into a living ritual with visual character and meaning.
The challenge

Most habit apps feel like spreadsheets in disguise: sterile cards, flat graphs, weak hierarchy, and emotionally empty progress loops.

What needed fixing
  • Typography lacked authority and character.
  • White content boxes broke the atmosphere.
  • Backgrounds felt plain and generic.
  • Charts looked like defaults rather than part of the brand.
  • The habit loop did not feel rewarding enough.
The opportunity

Create a ritual-based product where progress feels cultivated: grounded in mood, milestones, visual growth, and a premium editorial system.

Design objective

Build a habit product that users would want to return to for atmosphere and meaning, not only task completion.

Working idea

Habit tracking as a botanical studio: reflective, structured, visually alive, and premium in both light and dark mode.

People who want discipline without coldness: users who value self-awareness, beauty, and meaning in everyday systems.

Jobs to be done
  • Build consistency without feeling micromanaged.
  • See progress visually, not just numerically.
  • Tie habits to motivation, accountability, and reward.

Product pillars
  • Ritual over task
  • Reflection with momentum
  • Progress must be visible
  • Editing must be safe
  • Dark mode is first-class
  • Celebration must feel earned
Why this matters

Routiq is designed to feel like a companion system, not a compliance interface.

Milestone loop
  • User completes the current goal.
  • Reward is surfaced with celebration.
  • Next goal and reward are defined immediately.
  • Progress carries into the newly selected plant.
Plant logic

Rarer species require more effort, but every plant still communicates growth through a clean 12-stage visible system.

Why this works

The garden is both memory and motivation: it records effort already made while hinting at what can be cultivated next.
